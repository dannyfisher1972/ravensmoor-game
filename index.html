The Case

What Happened

Tonight was Edmund's seventieth birthday — and the night he'd promised to announce who would inherit the company, and what would happen to everything else. The storm took out the causeway bridge before dinner was even served. No police are coming for three more days.

At 11:47 PM, the housekeeper found him slumped at his study desk. Whether it was his heart, a terrible accident, or something else entirely is what you're here to find out.

Eleven people were in this house tonight. At least eight of them had a reason to want him gone.

The Victim

Edmund Thorne, 70 Founder & Chairman, Thorne Pharmaceutical Holdings

Edmund built Thorne Pharmaceutical Holdings from a single cardiac-drug patent into a four-hundred-million-pound company over forty years. He treated family loyalty as a debt to be collected on, and kept careful track of exactly what everyone around him owed.

Tonight he'd promised to announce changes to his will and the company's succession — changes he'd already discussed, in private, with five different family members. Each left his study looking as though they'd been given very different news.

He leaves behind a wife, two children, an ex-wife, a sister, a nephew, a business partner of thirty years, a lawyer of twelve, and a housekeeper who has run this house for twenty-six.

How to Play

  • Click a glowing object to examine it.
  • Click a person to talk to them.
  • Use the arrows at the top corners to move between rooms of the manor.
  • Go back and ask again — new questions open up for people you've already met once you've learned something elsewhere. Their portrait stays on the side panel so you don't have to walk back to find them.

Privacy (Beta)

          
Ravensmoor Hall runs entirely in your browser. This build has no account system, no backend server, and collects nothing that ever leaves your device.

          
What's stored, and where: every investigation, your Casebook, achievements, and your preferences are saved with your browser's local storage, on this device only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

          
Local stats ("My Stats"): a small local play-activity log stays on this device and is never transmitted, sold, or shared — it exists only to power your own My Stats screen.

          
Feedback & issue reports: Submit Beta Feedback copies a plain-text summary to your clipboard (when your browser allows it) and opens an external Google Form; Report an Issue opens your own share/email options instead. Nothing is sent automatically — only if you choose to paste it in and submit it yourself.

          
This policy describes the current local-only build. It will need to be rewritten if Ravensmoor Hall later adds a mobile app-store build, crash reporting, or any other form of remote telemetry.
