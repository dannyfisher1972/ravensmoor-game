# Ravensmoor Hall — Beta 1.1 Changelog

## New Features
- **Casebook** — track which of the 37 possible cases you've solved, with killer, method, and solve date revealed once you've actually cracked it.
- **Hint system** — stuck? A new Hint button offers a gentle nudge that escalates to a specific location or object if you need more help.
- **Evidence Strength Meter** — see how strong your case is against your selected suspect before you accuse.
- **Report an Issue** — a one-tap bug report tool that automatically grabs a screenshot and the relevant details, no explaining required.
- **Personal stats screen** — a simple Analytics view of your investigation history (cases started, solved, hint usage, and more).
- **Randomized investigations** — every fresh visit now rolls a brand-new case automatically; use the saved-investigations list to resume a specific one instead.

## Gameplay Changes
- Accusations are now capped at 2 attempts. A wrong first guess no longer spoils who the real killer is — only your final guess (right or wrong) reveals the solution.
- New questions now appear at the top of a character's question list instead of buried below.
- Added Edmund to the "Meet the Household" roster.

## Fixes
- Fixed several clues appearing on the wrong object after a full art-alignment pass across every room.
- Fixed closing a character's detail view accidentally closing the entire "Meet the Household" panel.
- Fixed modal close buttons scrolling out of reach on mobile.
- Fixed a mobile Safari bug where long dialogue could get clipped and unscrollable.
- Fixed dangling pronouns in several clue descriptions.
- Victoria's displayed name now correctly reflects her wife/girlfriend status everywhere it appears.

## Art & Content
- Updated Julian's and Nathaniel's portraits.
- Cropped Edmund's portrait to remove a stray text overlay.
- Clearer title-screen buttons: Resume Investigation vs. Start a New Investigation.
